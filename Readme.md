# Foundations of Limit-Centric Mathematics: Defining the Axiomatic Boundaries

This repository establishes a rigorous mathematical framework grounded strictly in **Constructive Processes** and **Limits**. It re-evaluates several foundational paradoxes of modern mathematics (such as those arising from ZFC set theory). Rather than viewing these paradoxes as profound limitations of human logic or counter-intuitive truths, this framework categorizes them naturally as **Undefined Operations**—mathematically invalid constructs akin to division by zero. 

By clearly formalizing these boundaries, we construct a consistent, contradiction-free mathematical universe. This system strictly prohibits the following three operations by defining them as mathematically invalid.

---

## 1. The Rejection of Actual Infinity (Cantor)
> **Principle: Treating an asymptotic process as a completed, measurable entity is an undefined operation.**

*   **Rationale:** Infinity represents a state of potential growth or a limit (Potential Infinity), not a static, completed domain (Actual Infinity). Tools such as bijective mappings (one-to-one correspondence) used to compare sizes are strictly valid only for finite sets. Applying finite measurement tools to never-ending processes is a categorical error.
*   **Consequences:** 
    *   **Infinite Sets:** The concept of an aggregated "infinite set" (e.g., the set of all real numbers) is undefined. However, individual limit values (e.g., √2, π) exist as static truths derived from well-defined rational sequences.
    *   **Cardinality & Continuum Hypothesis:** The notion of "different sizes of infinity" is based on the false premise of completed infinite sets. Thus, the Continuum Hypothesis is not an unsolved mystery, but a fundamentally undefined question.
    *   **Density over Bijection:** The statement "there are as many even numbers as natural numbers" is rejected. In this limit-centric framework, we rely on asymptotic natural density, yielding the rigorous conclusion that the proportion of even numbers approaches exactly 1/2.

## 2. The Rejection of Infinite Self-Reference (Gödel & Turing)
> **Principle: Self-referential propositions that generate paradoxes are not systemic limitations, but ill-formed syntax.**

*   **Rationale:** In standard arithmetic, the operation x/0 does not imply a collapse of mathematics; it is simply declared "undefined." Similarly, propositions constructed to refer to their own provability (Gödel numbering) or algorithms designed to predict and contradict their own halting state (Turing's Halting Problem) violate the hierarchical structure of logic. 
*   **Consequences:** 
    *   Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems and Turing's Halting Problem do not prove a fundamental flaw in formal logic. They merely demonstrate what happens when a system is fed an undefined, self-referential input.
    *   Paradoxes like Russell's Paradox are naturally resolved, as infinite self-containment is treated as an undefined grammatical error within the formal language.

## 3. The Rejection of the Axiom of Choice (Zermelo)
> **Principle: Mathematical existence without an explicit constructive rule is undefined.**

*   **Rationale:** The Axiom of Choice posits that one can select an element from an infinite collection of sets without an explicit rule or algorithm defining *how* the selection is made. In a strictly process-driven framework, every mathematical truth or object must be derived through a finite, explicit mechanism or its well-defined limit. Non-constructive existence—assuming a result exists from a "God's-eye view" without a definable process—is logically invalid.
*   **Consequences:** 
    *   Pathological geometric results, most notably the **Banach-Tarski Paradox** (which claims a solid sphere can be decomposed and reassembled into two identical spheres), are eliminated. These are not counter-intuitive physical or mathematical realities, but rather artifacts of applying the undefined Axiom of Choice to space.

## Conclusion: The Axiom of Objective Limits
The singular governing rule of this mathematical universe is as follows:

> **All mathematical objects and truths are strictly defined by finite algorithmic rules and the definite Limits they approach. Within these well-defined boundaries, the Law of Excluded Middle holds perfectly (every valid proposition is objectively true or false). Any proposition requiring completed infinities, self-reference, or non-constructive selection is not a paradox—it is simply Undefined.**

This framework provides a foundation where calculus, geometry, and algebra operate flawlessly, free from the artificial paradoxes introduced by actual infinities and self-referential loops.
